Figure 3. Modeled Slavic linguistic share at year 260 under the no-substrate (none) configuration of the agent-based model, with reverse_assimilation_rate = 0.030 and fortification_anchor_fraction = 0.30. Values are mean year-260 shares across 10 seed-42 model runs. Under the no-substrate null hypothesis, only the two source regions (Pannonian Plain, Lower Danubian Frontier) and a small Carpatho-Balkan Interior fraction retain measurable Slavic share; all four coastal destinations decay to near-zero. This is the configuration the parent submission's negative result corresponds to. Peloponnese has lower statistical resolution.
